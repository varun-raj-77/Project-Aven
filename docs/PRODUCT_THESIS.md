# Product thesis: hypotheses, not validation

Source: Master Description sections 17, 19-22. Aven is a working codename. No
market validation, external interviews, novelty claim, or product efficacy has
been established in AVEN-001.

## Proposed user and pain

Start with one owner. Possible later users are AI power users who repeatedly
re-teach context, switch model providers, or distrust agents that overstep.
Hypothesized pains: repeated corrections, stale or overly broad personalization,
lost historical episodes, broken continuity after model changes, and costly
oversight. These are assumptions to investigate, not facts about this workspace
owner.

## Proposed value and alternative

Less repetition, useful scoped defaults, inspectable evidence, portable owner
state, and bounded action authority. Minimal onboarding should collect only
necessary context, then support interaction, correction, and "Why did you do
that?". Thread/timeline views and proactivity are later capabilities, gated by
evidence.

The serious alternative is B: an editable scoped owner profile plus searchable
history on the same model. C must earn its additional ledger, typed state, and
governance complexity. A memory demo or an improvement over a fresh model does
not establish differentiation. Provider capabilities and market convergence must
be reassessed when making a future product decision; no current market claim is
made here.

## Evidence gates and kill criteria

- C materially beats B on held-out scoped tasks without unacceptable
  regressions.
- Gains do not depend mostly on extra labeling/review; net owner attention
  improves.
- Scope restraint, provenance, reversibility, disagreement preservation, and
  model continuity are observable rather than narrated.
- Later external users show recurring pull through concrete behavior, not
  hypothetical interest.

Investigate or simplify if C cannot beat B after honest iterations, complexity
has no measurable value, supervision becomes an attention tax, untrusted data
poisons state, or scope gains increase sycophancy/irrelevant personalization.
Market convergence and weak demand for portability/sovereignty are future
product kill criteria, not grounds to reopen today's selection debate without
evidence.

Public branding, domain/trademark checks, pricing, marketing, billing, startup
commitment, and scale are deferred.
